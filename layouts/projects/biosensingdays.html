About the Event

Biosensing Days brings together leading experts, researchers, and students to explore the latest breakthroughs and innovations in biosensor technology. This year's theme, Continuous Monitoring: Bridging Technology and Medicine, highlights the growing role of biosensors in advancing healthcare.

Speakers

Prof. Hatice Altug

EPFL — BIOS Laboratory
"Integrated Nanophotonics: Enabling Next-Generation Biosensor Systems for Life Science and Biomedical Applications"

Full Professor of Bioengineering and Director of the Doctoral School in Photonics at EPFL. Pioneer in nanophotonics developing high-sensitivity optical biosensors for real-time, label-free detection of biomolecules and pathogens.

Prof. Janos Vörös

ETH Zürich — Laboratory of Biosensors and Bioelectronics
"What limits diagnostically relevant biosensors?"

Full Professor in D-ITET at ETH Zürich. His work explores the interface between electronics and biology, specifically in "bottom-up neuroscience" and biosensors. His lab has shaped the careers of over 700 alumni.

Dr. Noé Brasier

ETH Zürich — MedLab Fellow
"Wearable Devices in Clinical Medicine: Navigating a Paradigm Shift"

Medical doctor specialized in general internal medicine and physician-scientist at ETH Zürich. Research focuses on the translation of wearable bodyfluid analysis into clinical application.

Felix Kurth

CSEM — Biosensing Technologies Group Leader
"Next generation biosensing solutions and challenges in biofluids monitoring"

CSEM is a Swiss R&T organization bridging fundamental research and industry, with strong applications in healthcare including wearable and implantable sensing systems.

Arsalan Saeedi

Sentec
Biosensing and continuous patient monitoring

Sentec is a Swiss medical technology company specializing in advanced monitoring solutions, including electrochemical and transcutaneous systems for continuous, non-invasive monitoring of physiological parameters.

Benoît Schaller

Medtronic
"Medtronic: Engineering the Future"

Medtronic is an international leader in medical technologies, designing innovative devices for cardiology, neurology, diabetes, and minimally invasive surgery. Present in more than 150 countries.
