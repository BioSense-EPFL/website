## About the Hackathon

Biosensing is most commonly understood as detecting a chemical compound in a liquid medium, usually with the goal of detecting an illness. We understand it more broadly — as the act of **identifying a biological marker**, be it chemical, imaging, or even behavioral.

In this spirit, we are organizing the **first ever biosensing hackathon in Lausanne**.

## Stay Tuned

The hackathon is in active planning. Follow us on social media for updates on dates, registration, and challenges!
